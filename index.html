How to Play Rock Paper Scissors (Stone Paper Scissors)

Rock Paper Scissors, also known as Stone Paper Scissors, is a classic hand game. Pick rock, paper or scissors, and your opponent picks at the same time. Whoever's choice beats the other wins the round. You can play online with a friend by sharing a room code, or play against the computer.

The Rules

  • Rock (stone) beats scissors.
  • Scissors beats paper.
  • Paper beats rock (stone).
  • If both players pick the same move, it is a tie and you play again.

Game Modes

Play Online: enter a room code and share it with a friend. Log in to track your wins and climb the leaderboard.

Play Computer: choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Insane and see how long you can keep winning.

Tips to Win More Often

People tend to repeat patterns. Mix up your moves, avoid always starting with the same choice, and watch what your opponent played last round.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Stone Paper Scissors the same as Rock Paper Scissors?
Yes. The rules are identical. "Stone" and "rock" are two names for the same game.

Is it free to play?
Yes, it is completely free and runs in your browser with no download.

Can I play on my phone?
Yes, it works on phones, tablets and computers.
