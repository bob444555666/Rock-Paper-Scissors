How to play

Each turn gives you up to 3 rolls. Keep dice you like by tapping them, then reroll the rest. Bank the sum of all five dice whenever you want. The computer takes its turn after you bank. First to 100 wins.

Match strategy

Lock high dice to protect points. If your total is already strong, bank early rather than risk rerolling. Your best score and match stats are saved on this device.
